## 🛠️ MESHBULLET Assembly Instructions

### 1. Prerequisites & Safety
Before starting the assembly, ensure you have a clean workspace, basic metric hex keys, and proper ventilation if using adhesives.

### 2. Component Inspection
Lay out all components and inspect the 3D-printed ABS parts for dimensional accuracy. ABS offers excellent mechanical strength and heat resistance, making it perfect for the high-stress areas of the MESHBULLET frame.

### 3. Step-by-Step Build Sequence

#### Step A: Frame Preparation
*   Clean out any residual support material from the printed internal channels.
*   Insert the required threaded brass inserts into the designated ABS mounting points using a soldering iron set to low heat.

#### Step B: Core Core Component Integration
*   Align the main hardware assembly with the alignment rails printed into the internal housing.
*   Secure the base framework using metric machine screws. Do not overtighten to prevent stripping the plastic threads.

#### Step C: Final Enclosure Fitment
*   Snap the upper ABS shell over the completed assembly core.
*   Fasten the remaining retention screws through the exterior brackets to lock the system into place.
